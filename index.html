MeadMath

Honest mead math. How much honey, what the yeast will really do to it, when it finishes, and what a bottle of it costs you in patience.

Honey by gravity, not vibes. Honey contributes about 35 gravity points per pound per gallon. Tell it the batch size and target gravity; it tells you the pounds and the water that actually fits around them.
The yeast's opinion counts. Bread yeast quits around 12%, EC-1118 keeps eating to 18%. Your finishing gravity - dry or sweet - is the yeast's decision, and this shows it before you pitch.
Hydrometer honesty. Warm must reads low, cold must reads high. Temperature-corrected readings, a staggered nutrient schedule, and a backsweetening dose with the stabilizer warning that skips bottle bombs.
